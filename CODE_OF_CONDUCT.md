# Code of Conduct

Saylacian project spaces aim to be respectful, accessible and focused on preservation. Participate in good faith. Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, unwanted disclosure of private information and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Critique code, evidence and claims without attacking people. Respect language communities' naming and orthographic preferences; do not present uncertain mappings as authoritative. Do not share another person's private messages, identity or unpublished language data without permission.

The project has no public reporting channel yet. Until one is established, raise concerns privately with the project owner through the channel where the project was shared. No enforcement process or legal entity is implied by this document.
