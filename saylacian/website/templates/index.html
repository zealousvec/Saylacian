Support and privacy

Somali Latin / Osmanya conversion is experimental. English ↔ French and English ↔ Somali meaning models run locally; Somali ↔ French uses an experimental two-step route through English. Somali output needs fluent human review, and two-step errors may compound. Borama, Kaddare and Nubian conversions remain unavailable.
