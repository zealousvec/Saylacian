# Nubian variety research register

Old Nubian is a historical written language. It must not be used as a proxy for modern Nubian languages or their writing systems.

| Variety | Identity / orthography evidence | Saylacian state | Safe next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| Old Nubian | Historical language; app has a cited 29-character Coptic-derived reading table | Limited character reading and reverse parsing; no meaning translation | Keep the source attribution visible and request specialist review before expanding readings |
| Nobiin | Distinct modern language; Latin orthography research exists, with community-specific practice | No implemented route; hidden from normal selector | Compare documented orthographies and licensed text resources; do not reuse Old Nubian rules |
| Kenzi / Mattokki | Distinct modern variety, often discussed with Kenuzi/Dongolawi; naming/classification must cite source | No implemented route; hidden from normal selector | Establish preferred language name and orthography from primary/community sources |
| Dongolawi / Andaandi | Distinct modern variety; do not merge with Nobiin | No implemented route; hidden from normal selector | Identify source-backed orthography and dataset rights separately |
| Midob | Distinct modern Nubian variety | No implemented route; hidden from normal selector | Research its own orthography, resources and reviewer requirements |
| Birgid | Distinct modern variety with limited documentation | No implemented route; hidden from normal selector | Research independently; avoid extrapolating from neighboring varieties |
| Hill Nubian varieties | Multiple varieties, not a single route | No implemented routes; hidden from normal selector | Register each variety separately before considering any implementation |

## Sources to verify and extend

- [Nobiin orthography study](https://www.lddjournal.org/article/1214/galley/2458/download/): research reference; verify orthographic details and reuse rights before deriving mappings.
- [Starostin, “Restoring Nile-Nubian”](https://escholarship.org/content/qt6663f1nt/qt6663f1nt.pdf?t=qozj86): comparative classification resource; not a writing-standard specification.
- [Glottolog Kenuzi-Dongola record](https://glottolog.org/resource/languoid/id/kenu1236) and [WALS Andaandi record](https://wals.info/languoid/lect/wals_code_nbd): language identity/classification references, not permission to redistribute data.
- [Unicode Coptic block](https://unicode.org/charts/PDF/U2C80.pdf): code-point evidence for Old Nubian letters, not modern Nubian orthography.

No route is enabled until a variety-specific orthography, appropriately licensed/reusable source data, implementation, tests and independent community or expert review are documented. Keep unavailable varieties out of the ordinary picker.
