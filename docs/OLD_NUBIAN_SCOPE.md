# Old Nubian reading scope

Saylacian provides limited character transliteration between selected Coptic-derived Old Nubian characters and scholarly Latin readings. It does not translate meaning, provide a complete alphabet, interpret manuscripts, or support modern Nubian varieties such as Nobiin, Kenzi/Mattokki, Dongolawi/Andaandi or Midob.

The current table contains 29 character readings from the peer-reviewed 2024 *UCLA Encyclopedia of Egyptology* overview. Its **Table 1** lists each character's scholarly Latin transliteration separately from its phonetic values; the letter reference now displays both fields. Unknown characters are preserved with a warning. Reverse Latin-to-script conversion can be ambiguous, especially for `ou`, which can represent `ⲟⲩ` or `ⳣ`. Saylacian preserves combining marks, offers an `i`-based alternative for the supralinear stroke, and shows cited phonetic alternatives for `ⲏ`, `ⲩ`, `ⲉⲓ` and `ⲟⲩ`. Alternative lists are capped at 12. These readings do not interpret manuscripts or resolve differences between editions. A fluent Old Nubian specialist has not reviewed the implementation.

The bundled table no longer uses the previous DBMNT-derived mapping; its table-redistribution blocker is resolved. Specialist review remains outstanding. Saylacian does not include manuscript passages or a word lexicon from the separately licensed Old Nubian edition. Any future use of DBMNT material still needs written permission.
