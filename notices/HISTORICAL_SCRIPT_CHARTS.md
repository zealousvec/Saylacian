Saylacian adaptations include the chart-label data in `saylacian/core/data/scripts/borama.json` and `kaddare.json`, and the bitmap-derived PUA display fonts `assets/BoramaChart-Regular.ttf` and `assets/KaddareChart-Regular.ttf`. The forms were cropped/transcribed from the charts and the font outlines generated by `tools/build_chart_fonts.py`; the Kaddare font includes separate private-use slots for the chart's uppercase and lowercase cursive forms. The assignment of the two Kaddare chart forms to upper and lower case follows the chart notes at [Omniglot](https://www.omniglot.com/writing/kaddare.htm) and SIL's description of cursive lowercase at [Writing Systems Technical Resources](https://writingsystems.info/scrlang/scripts/x-kaddare/). These are not standard Unicode encodings or validated word-level orthography. Attribute Jugydmort and link to the chart and CC BY-SA 4.0 licence when distributing these adaptations. The adaptations are provided under CC BY-SA 4.0; changes and extraction are described here. No endorsement is implied.

The chart fonts can be rebuilt with the pinned Pillow and FontTools dependencies in `requirements-chart-font-build.txt`, using `python tools/build_chart_fonts.py --script Kaddare` or `--script Borama`.

The rebuilt case-aware Kaddare font SHA-256 is `A056AD0D1D77E832AE29BFE082A9B81446E8048387F3CBD4F0115A5C871E41BA`.
