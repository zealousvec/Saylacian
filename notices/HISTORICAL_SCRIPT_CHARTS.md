Saylacian adaptations include the chart-label data in `saylacian/core/data/scripts/borama.json` and `kaddare.json`, and the bitmap-derived PUA display fonts `assets/BoramaChart-Regular.ttf` and `assets/KaddareChart-Regular.ttf`. The forms were cropped/transcribed from the charts and the font outlines generated by `tools/build_chart_fonts.py`; they are not standard Unicode encodings or a validated word-level orthography. Attribute Jugydmort and link to the chart and CC BY-SA 4.0 licence when distributing these adaptations. The adaptations are provided under CC BY-SA 4.0; changes and extraction are described here.
